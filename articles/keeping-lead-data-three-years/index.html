A lead collected by your chat today can be kept until 25 September 2029

The CNIL’s guidance lets businesses keep prospect data for three years from collection or the prospect’s last contact. How to apply that to leads from a website chat.

Three years, counted from the prospect’s last move, not yours

1,096 days

the CNIL’s three-year guideline for a lead collected on 25 September 2026, unless the prospect gets back in touch

The CNIL says prospect data can be kept for three years from collection or from the last contact coming from the prospect, such as a reply or a click on a link. An email you send does not restart the clock, and neither does the prospect simply opening it.

25 September 2026 + 3 years = 25 September 2029, i.e. 1,096 days (2028 is a leap year)

Source: CNIL, Q&A on the référentiels for managing commercial activities, 28 January 2022.

CNIL référentiels are guidance, not binding rules, and a lead who becomes a client falls under client-data rules instead. Not legal advice.

Every contact your chat collects is personal data. The GDPR requires you to set a retention period, and the CNIL gives a clear benchmark for prospects.

The CNIL’s benchmark

In its guidance on managing commercial activities, the CNIL says prospect data can be kept for three years from collection or from the last contact coming from the prospect. It gives a click on a link in an email as an example of such contact. Its sanction decisions add that simply opening an email does not count.

What that means for a lead from your chat

SituationKeep until
Lead collected on 25 September 2026, no further contact25 September 2029

The lead becomes a clientClient-data rules apply

Four good habits

  • Date every lead when it arrives, and record the prospect’s last contact.
  • Review once a year and delete leads older than three years without any contact from them.
  • Keep the conversation only as long as needed: the chat transcript does not need to outlive the lead.
  • Say it up front: tell visitors how long you keep their details, in the chat and in your privacy policy.
