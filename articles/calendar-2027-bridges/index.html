2027 has two more working days than 2026, and two 112-hour bridge weekends

Four public holidays fall at the weekend in 2027, giving 254 working days. But Ascension Day and 11 November fall on a Thursday: take the Friday off and your business is closed 112 hours in a row.

Omni calculation

A Thursday holiday plus a bridge day makes the longest closure of the year

112 h

closed in a row from Wednesday 5 pm to Monday 9 am if you take the Friday after Ascension Day (6 May 2027) or 11 November 2027

In 2027, 1 May, 8 May and Christmas fall on a Saturday and 15 August on a Sunday, so only 7 public holidays fall on a weekday. That gives 254 working days, two more than 2026. Ascension Day and 11 November both fall on a Thursday.

Wednesday 5 pm → Monday 9 am = 4 × 24 h + 16 h = 112 h

2027 calendar; French public holidays (Service-Public.fr); Easter Sunday 2027 on 28 March.

Bridge days are not public holidays: whether you close is your choice. Without the bridge, the longest 2027 closures last 88 hours, as in 2026.

Planning 2027 starts with the calendar. The good news: more working days. The trap: two Thursdays that invite a long weekend.

In 2027, 1 May, 8 May and Christmas Day fall on a Saturday, and 15 August on a Sunday. Easter Monday is 29 March, Ascension Day 6 May and Whit Monday 17 May.

The two bridges

Ascension Day (Thursday 6 May) and Armistice Day (Thursday 11 November) invite a bridge on the Friday. With the bridge, the office closes on Wednesday at 5 pm and reopens on Monday at 9 am: 112 hours without an answer. Without it, the longest closures are the 88-hour long weekends, such as Easter or All Saints’ Day.

Prepare now

  • Decide your bridges early and put them on your site.
  • Check who answers during each long weekend.
  • Plan the Monday after: that is when four days of enquiries arrive at once.
